# Code of Conduct

This project is small and run by one person in spare time. The goal is simple: everyone who takes part, in an issue, a pull request or a discussion, can do so without being harassed or put down.

## What we expect

- Be respectful. Disagree with the code or the idea, never with the person.
- Assume good faith, and say what you mean plainly and kindly.
- Welcome newcomers. Questions are fine, and so is not speaking English or French perfectly.
- Keep to the subject of the project in its issues and pull requests.

## What is not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, threats, or personal attacks.
- Discrimination or demeaning comments about a person's origin, gender, orientation, disability, age, religion or appearance.
- Publishing someone else's private information.
- Spam, or repeatedly pushing a change after the maintainer has said no.

## What happens

The maintainer can edit or remove comments, close or lock issues and pull requests, and block people who do not follow these rules, without warning when the behaviour is serious.

## Reporting

To report a problem, use GitHub's private reporting: open the repository's **Security** tab, choose **Report a vulnerability**, and say it is a conduct report. Reports are read only by the maintainer and kept private.

This text applies in the project's spaces, and when someone represents the project in public.
